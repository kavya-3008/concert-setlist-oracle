Concert Setlist Oracle

Tours are more regular than they look: a core set every night, plus a few rotating slots. This predicts the next setlist from past shows and flags which songs are "due" as a surprise.

Demo data is a synthetic tour by a fictional band (36 shows). Source on GitHub.

Where do the misses come from?

The recency model recovers 76% of played songs (pooled over 31 shows). Only songs never played earlier are out of reach, and that caps recovery at 99%; just 3% of misses are of that kind. The rest are rotation errors: songs it knew about but left out.
